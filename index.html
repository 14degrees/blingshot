Questions

The screen stays black or says camera access is off

Open Settings, find Blingshot, and turn on Camera. Then open Blingshot again.

How do I hold it?

Sideways, like a real camera. The big round pad takes the photo, the rocker zooms, and the small button opens your roll.

How do I use the flash?

Tap the lightning mark on the right side of the round pad to turn it on or off. The icon on the screen shows which it is. Not every iPhone can fire its flash with RAW photos.

Where do my photos go?

Each finished photo is saved to your Photos library, if you allow it. Blingshot also keeps its own copy in the app, with the original RAW and your iPhone's version, so you can compare them.

My photos aren't showing up in Photos

Open Settings, find Blingshot, and allow it to add photos. Photos you already took stay in the app; open one, tap MENU and share it to save it.

If I delete a photo, is it gone everywhere?

Deleting in Blingshot removes the app's copies. The one in your Photos library stays until you delete it there. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

How much space does it use?

About 13 MB for each photo, because the app keeps the RAW. Delete shots from the roll to get space back.

Which iPhones work?

Any iPhone with iOS 17 or later whose main camera can capture RAW photos. If yours can't, the app tells you.

Why do my photos look a bit soft and hazy?

That's the idea. Cameras from 2008 had small soft lenses and heavy processing, and Blingshot develops your RAW the same way.
