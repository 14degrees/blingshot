Privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Blingshot does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Everything happens on your iPhone. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network connections.

Camera

Blingshot uses your camera to take photos. It turns each one into a finished picture on your phone.

Photos

With your permission, each finished photo is added to your photo library. Blingshot can only add photos. It cannot see or change the ones already there.

The app also keeps its own copy of each photo, the original RAW file and your iPhone's version of the same shot. These stay in the app's storage on your phone until you delete them or delete the app. If you back up your iPhone, your backup may include them, and Apple's privacy terms cover that backup.

When you share a photo, it goes only where you choose to send it.

Location

Blingshot never asks for your location and does not add it to your photos. Your photos do record ordinary camera details, such as the date, time and exposure.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us crash reports and usage numbers that don't identify you. You can turn this off in Settings.

Children

Blingshot is suitable for everyone and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If any of this changes, we will update this page before the change ships.
